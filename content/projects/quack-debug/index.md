### The Problem
Traditional debugging often requires "Rubber Ducking," but physical ducks don't integrate with a digital workflow.

### The Solution
A native macOS utility built with **SwiftUI** and **AppKit**. 
* **Always-on-Top:** Floating panel that sits over VS Code/Xcode.
* **Local-First:** Speech-to-text processing stays on your machine.
* **Menu Bar Extra:** Quick access to toggle the session.
